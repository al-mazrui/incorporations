Why IFCO?

A purpose-built jurisdiction designed for international finance, wealth structuring, and cross-border commerce.

Common Law Framework

English law principles govern contracts, corporate structures, and dispute resolution. Familiar, predictable, internationally recognised.

50-Year Tax Exemption

Eligible activities exempt from corporate income tax. Non-Omani individuals exempt from personal income tax on IFCO earnings.

Enforceable Judgments

IFCO court orders enforced throughout Oman without re-examination. Your judgments have teeth across the Sultanate.

Geopolitical Neutrality

Oman's historic neutrality makes IFCO a safe harbour for entities sensitive to regional polarisation.

Lower Operating Costs

Competitive licensing fees and office costs. Access international standards without Dubai pricing.

Full Compliance

AML/CFT, criminal law, and tax frameworks apply. FATF and OECD compliant from inception.
